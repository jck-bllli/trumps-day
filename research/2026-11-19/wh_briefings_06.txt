					Presidential Message on National Native American Heritage Month				

	The White House

November 12, 2026					

Alaska Natives and Native Americans are inextricably linked to the history of our Republic and are integral to our glorious future.  During National Native American Heritage Month, we honor the sacrifices and the innumerable contributions of tribal communities to the prosperity and success of our great Nation. 

Native Americans impact communities nationwide with innovation and achievement in every area of life and culture, including business, healthcare, law enforcement, and the highest levels of Government.  They bravely answer the call to safeguard our liberty—at five times the national average—and have served with distinction in every major conflict since the Revolutionary War. This month, we pay special tribute to the Indigenous men and women who have proudly worn the uniform, past and present, in every branch of our Armed Forces.

As President, I support the empowerment of all Tribes through self-governance, self-determination, and a strong partnership with the Federal Government to foster economic development and address the most pressing challenges for our Native Americans.  Last year, I returned 680 acres of federal land to the Spirit Lake Nation of North Dakota—a long-overdue restoration—now theirs to build on, farm on, and pass down to future generations.  I also directed more than $1.1 billion in Indian Housing Block Grants to hundreds of Tribes, Alaska Native Villages, and Tribally Designated Housing Entities for safe and stable homes in Native communities nationwide.  In January, the Bureau of Indian Education reached its highest graduation rate in its history, reflecting sustained reforms, accountability measures, and quality instruction to bolster student achievement.  My Administration will continue to prioritize educational options, training, and meaningful pathways to the workforce of tomorrow so that Native students and communities can enjoy a bright and successful future.  

As our Nation marks National Native American Heritage Month, we celebrate the vibrant traditions and rich cultural heritage of Tribal peoples.  We uphold the shared values of family, faith, and freedom, and we will never stop fighting to ensure every Native American has the opportunity to pursue the hope and promise of the American Dream.
